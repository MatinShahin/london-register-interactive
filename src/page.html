Data

The Register in full

Annual reported mass of CO2 stored per project, in million tonnes. Shading scales with the amount. The full database, with sources and quantification protocols for every value, is on Zenodo.

Definitions

Geological storage
CO2 injected into saline formations or depleted fields with the primary purpose of storing it.
Geological storage with oil recovery
CO2 injected into producing oil fields, where it improves recovery and a share is retained underground.
Mt
Million tonnes of CO2. One gigatonne (Gt) is 1,000 Mt.

Calculations in this edition

Totals and shares
Sums of the project values published in the Register. Missing years count as zero.
Start cohort
Grouped by the first year a project reports storage above zero.
Growth fit
Least-squares line through the natural log of annual totals, 2005–2024.

Reading the data

2025 is incomplete
Several operators had not reported 2025 when the edition was compiled.
Repeated values
— projects report an identical value for four or more consecutive years. The Register notes that where a project reports only a cumulative amount, the average is used for each year, and its spreadsheet marks those cases.
Protocols
Per-project sources and protocols are in the full database and the methodology report.

Source, citation and credits

The Register is maintained at Imperial College London. This interactive edition presents its published data and is not an official Imperial College London website.

The Register

A centralised register of annual CO2 stored underground by operational projects, built from operator submissions and public reporting. Funded by the Royal Academy of Engineering and supported by a consortium from academia, industry and government.

What this interactive edition adds

The official website and annual reports remain the source for the data, the narrative findings, the share-by-project chart, the project-level Figure 1 and the reporting-standards timeline. This edition adds interactive views of the same published data:

  • Year-on-year bridge showing project by project how the total changed, for any pair of years
  • 2025 like-for-like comparison and a list of the largest projects still to report
  • Reporting coverage chart showing every project's history and highlighting repeated annual values
  • Start-cohort breakdown of annual storage by when projects began reporting
  • Project histories for every project, with side-by-side comparison of up to four
  • Country map with each country's split by storage type
  • Growth scenario to 2050 you can adjust, labelled as illustrative
  • Light and dark themes on a documented design system
